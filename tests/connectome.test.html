The Connectome · express(occupants, ascSignIdx, sect, meta) → a frozen Expression

The join: occupants (the same sign map dispositor.walk takes) + a Tympan selector (a sign address, or null for a placeless field) + sect (true/false/null) compile to an Expression. No longitude, no degree, no retrograde on a node — sign resolution, by law. The house-routing graph is the twin of the planet graph, built here (own-house · house-exchange · routing-loop), deliberately NOT sharing the planet graph's words (domicile · mutual-reception · dispositor-loop) because they name different relations.
