Phase 6 · P2 + P3 + P4 — the prism's tables, its ascension template and its return dial

The synchronic layer is a whole other timespine, calculated once and then displayed. §1 is why that is literally true: sASC = midpoint(nASC, rising degree) contains no time, so a synchronic day is a fixed template plus one number. These checks pin the template. Nothing here stores a position (the refraction stays arithmetic, one wrap and one halving) and nothing here stores an event time — what is stored is the structure a live cursor is read through.

P3 adds the day's other half. The itinerary is uniform in DEGREES and the day is not uniform in TIME: the seven stretches are the ascension times of their arcs at the native's latitude, so they run wildly unequal and stay that way for a life. These checks measure the template against a live horizon root-find, and the anchor's regression against sidereal arithmetic over two years. A DURATION HAS NO EPOCH, which is why storing one is not storing an event.
