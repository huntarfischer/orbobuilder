# Orbo Rectifier: Ascendant-Ruler House Question Bank

**Version:** 1.0  
**Framework:** Traditional planetary rulers, whole-sign houses  
**Inventory:** 12 rising signs × 12 possible houses for the Ascendant ruler = **144 questions**

## Purpose

This bank tests the **house occupied by the traditional ruler of the candidate Ascendant**. It does not attempt to prove a rising sign from generic personality traits. Each question asks whether the candidate chart ruler repeatedly expresses itself through the life topics of one whole-sign house.

These questions belong to Orbo’s `FRAME_SEARCH` stage. They should be used only after stronger temporal evidence, especially time-of-day bounds and any Moon-sign branch, has reduced the candidate set. Frame evidence should remain capped and should not outweigh dated events.

## Standard answer set: `FRAME4`

| Code | User-facing answer | Suggested score |
|---|---|---:|
| A | This has been a strong, recurring pattern. | 1.00 |
| B | This fits partly or during important periods. | 0.35 |
| C | This has rarely or never described my life. | -0.75 |
| D | I am unsure or do not have enough evidence. | 0.00 |

The score is evidence for or against the specific candidate frame named by the row. Apply the score at low weight and cap the total contribution of the personality/frame evidence family.

## Pull order

1. **Phase A — Angular:** houses 1, 4, 7, 10. These describe the most visible life architecture and should be sampled first.
2. **Phase B — Succedent:** houses 2, 5, 8, 11. These describe resources, continuity, creation, entanglement, and gains.
3. **Phase C — Cadent:** houses 3, 6, 9, 12. These describe learning, labor, worldview, retreat, and less immediately visible patterns.

Do not ask all twelve questions for one sign in a normal orientation. Pull only from surviving candidate frames, rotate evidence families, and allow `D` to remain genuinely neutral.

## Implementation cautions

- Treat a response as evidence about a **candidate frame**, not as a diagnosis of character.
- Prefer recurring biographical patterns over what the user merely likes, admires, or wishes were true.
- Avoid presenting two questions whose wording makes one answer seem more admirable.
- Rephrase or skip questions that depend on adult experiences the user has not yet had.
- Never use frame questions to overrule a strong dated-event match, a resolved Moon-sign branch, or a hard time-of-day boundary.
- Use Sabian-symbol prompts only at the final degree-refinement stage, after the rising sign and frame are already strongly supported.
