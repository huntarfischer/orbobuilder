# I. FIELD

## Field

**Core term.** An astrological configuration of light/energy resolved into chart-like structure.

> **A chart is already a Field.**

A Field does not require a second chart to make it a Field. Relation can produce a new derived Field, but the participating Fields already exist before the relation.

Examples:

```text
natal chart
moment chart
relationship composite
synchronic composite
company origin chart
historical event chart
```

all represent Fields of different kinds.

**Supersedes v1:** the definition of Field as only a relational environment generated by two or more structures.

---

## Physical Field

A Field whose celestial state physically existed at a declared temporal/spatial address.

Examples:

```text
birth sky
email-send moment
execution moment
current sky
company founding moment
```

A physical Field's canonical celestial state is minted through the Embryo as AstroDNA.

---

## Derived Field

A Field produced by a declared transformation or relation between one or more Fields.

Examples:

```text
relationship composite
synchronic composite
progressed/derived state
other provenance-marked transformations
```

A derived Field must retain provenance and must not claim that its derived placements physically occupied the sky.

---

## Moment Field

The physical celestial Field at a specified temporal address, with a declared horizon/place where the reading requires one.

`M(t)` denotes the Moment Field at temporal coordinate `t`.

A Moment Field may be current, historical, future, prenatal relative to a person, or posthumous relative to a person. Those are relationships to a subject, not different astronomical species.

---

## Natal Field

The physical Field crystallized at birth.

The natal Field is the native's fixed constitutional Field and the identity coordinate of that native's Synchronic Spine.

For birth moment `b`:

```text
M(b) = A
```

where `A` is the natal Field.

---

## Relationship Field

A derived Field produced by the relationship-composite operation between persistent Fields, conventionally two natal Fields.

```text
AB = Composite(A,B)
```

The relationship Field is different from the Synchronic Synastry relation between A and B's separate synchronic timelines.

---

## Field State

The complete condition of a Field at one specified temporal coordinate/resolution.

Depending on the Field and reader, this can include:

- positions;
- houses/frame references;
- rulership/governance;
- aspects/relations;
- motion;
- applying/separating;
- sect;
- Lots;
- boundary proximity;
- timing conditions;
- other Connectome expression.

A Field State is a snapshot of motion, not a claim that the underlying Field is motionless.

---

## Field Encounter

The relation/operation by which one Field is combined with or compared to another.

A Field Encounter may create a new derived Field or preserve two Fields and compare them.

Examples:

```text
Composite(A,B)
  creates derived relationship Field

Composite(A,M(t))
  creates derived synchronic Field

Synastry(A,B)
  compares two Fields without merging them
```

**Amends v1:** the participants are themselves Fields or Field-bearing states, not non-field objects waiting for relation to create fieldhood.

# II. CRYSTALLIZATION AND CRYSTALS

## Crystallization

**Core term.** The act of resolving/fixing a continuously moving Field at a temporal address so its state becomes addressable.

```text
continuous Field
      |
      | resolve at t
      v
Field State at t
```

Crystallization does not necessarily mean persistence. Orbo may crystallize a state for transient computation and discard it, or retain it because the time matters.

A Crystal can preserve motion information. Fixing an instant does not erase the velocities and relational directions present at that instant.

---

## Crystal

**Core term.** A Field State fixed to a temporal address so it can be retained, named, selected, linked, compared, returned to, or used as a hinge.

A retained Crystal may carry/reference:

```text
temporal address
place / horizon provenance
AstroDNA where physical
source confidence
Field links
trigger/event family
temporal role
annotations/evidence links
```

A Crystal does not manufacture the Synchronic Spine. It identifies a coordinate on temporal structures that already exist.

---

## Crystallized Moment

The fuller retention phrase for a Crystal whose defining fact is one temporal coordinate.

Examples:

```text
birth
death
email sent
reply received
first hearing
ritual hinge
exact perfection
historical event
future election
Horary Lock
```

---

## Candidate Crystal

A temporal coordinate being investigated or searched but not yet accepted/retained as the chosen moment.

Examples:

- conception hypothesis;
- possible historical birth event;
- future election candidate;
- suspected historical hinge.

Candidate status is provenance, not lesser astronomy.

# III. SYNCHRONIC

## Synchronic

**Core coined qualifier.** Describes a Field/state produced by relating a Field to a Moment Field through celestial time.

**Synchronic does not mean only NOW or live/current.**

Past, present, future, prenatal, and posthumous temporal coordinates can all produce synchronic states.

NOW is simply the current coordinate on the Synchronic Spine.

**Supersedes v1 wording:** synchronic as primarily present-tense relation to the present moment.

---

## Synchronic Field

For persistent Field `A` and Moment Field `M(t)`:

```text
S_A(t) = Composite(A, M(t))
```

or under Orbo's refraction vocabulary:

```text
S_A(t) = Refract(A, M(t))
```

`S_A(t)` is the synchronic Field state of `A` at temporal coordinate `t`.

It is a derived Field.

---

## Synchronic Composite

The chart representation/sample of `S_A(t)` at one temporal coordinate.

A Synchronic Composite is not definitionally "always live." It may be calculated at any supported past, present, or future `t`.

**Supersedes older Ledger wording:** Synchronic Composite as intrinsically live/current while Minted Composite is static.

---

## Synchronic Spine

**Core temporal term.** The temporal extension/function of a Field's synchronic states.

```text
S_A(t) = Composite(A, M(t))
```

for every supported `t`.

The Synchronic Spine is conceptually continuous across Orbo's temporal model.

A materialized spine is only the practical cache/index of useful samples, intervals, crossings, and requested coverage.

> **The Synchronic Spine is the astrology of a Field as a continuous temporal object.**

---

## Birth Identity Coordinate

The special point on a natal Field's Synchronic Spine where the Moment Field equals the natal Field.

```text
S_A(birth)
= Composite(A,A)
= A
```

Birth anchors the Synchronic Spine but does not bound its temporal reach.

---

## Biographical Span

The interval during which a human native is physically alive/embodied.

This is distinct from Synchronic extent.

```text
birth ================= death
```

The natal Field may still be related to Moment Fields before birth or after death.

---

## Synchronic Extent

The range of temporal coordinates over which a Field can be related to Moment Fields.

Conceptually this is broader than biography.

Computationally it is bounded by the Embryo/ephemeris domain that the shipped Orbo version supports.

---

## Synchronic Clock

The user-facing playhead/transport over a Synchronic Spine.

> **The Synchronic Clock is the playhead on the Synchronic Spine.**

It is not merely a current-weather indicator or date picker.

Conceptual operations include:

```text
NOW
forward
backward
jump to Crystal
jump to Intersection
return to birth identity
scrub through temporal handles
```

Moving the Field through time and moving time through the Field are equivalent descriptions of the same relation.

---

## Return Grid

A useful sampling/indexing coordinate on a Synchronic Spine, such as Phase 7's birth-anchored rising-return grid.

The grid does not define the Field's existence.

A synchronic state exists at supported coordinates between anchors as well.

# IV. SYNCHRONIC SYNASTRY AND INTERSECTIONS

## Shared Moment

The same Moment Field `M(t)` used to evaluate multiple persistent Fields at one temporal coordinate.

Under the founding Synchronic Synastry practice:

```text
S_A(t) = Composite(A,M(t))
S_B(t) = Composite(B,M(t))
```

Both sides use the same moment.

The unresolved horizon/locality variants remain a separate reconciliation issue. Shared temporal coordinate is settled.

---

## Synchronic Synastry

**Core coined term.** The synastry between two synchronic Fields evaluated at the same temporal coordinate.

```text
R_AB(t)
=
Synastry(
  S_A(t),
  S_B(t)
)
```

This preserves the two synchronic Fields rather than averaging them into a three-way composite.

---

## Synchronic Synastry Spine

The continuous relational chronology/function:

```text
R_AB(t)
```

across supported synchronic time.

It is the crossing of two personal synchronic timelines.

This is the temporal object that the earlier Orbo working name **Intersections** was trying to describe.

---

## Intersection

A meaningful exact/threshold relational event on a Synchronic Synastry Spine.

Examples:

- cross-body aspect ingress;
- perfection;
- egress;
- applying/exact/separating transition where useful;
- mode shift;
- Lot contact;
- shared-governor/chain convergence;
- boundary crossing;
- other doctrine-admitted exact relation event.

`Intersections` was previously used in a Ledger spec for exact dated events surfaced from continual Synchronic Synastry computation. The term remains valuable at the event level.

---

## Same-Body Synchronic Relation

The relation between the same synchronic function in two Fields, such as Moon-to-Moon or Venus-to-Venus.

Because the shared Moment term cancels structurally, these relations possess stable aspect-family geometry with mode/phase behavior.

The existing v1 definitions of shared-sky cancellation, structural backbone, locked aspect, and complementary aspect family remain useful unless later mathematical review changes them.

---

## Cross-Body Synchronic Relation

The relation between different synchronic functions across two Fields, such as A's Moon to B's Mars.

The two Moment terms do not cancel because the underlying celestial bodies move differently.

These relations can:

- enter orb;
- apply;
- perfect;
- separate;
- leave orb;
- reform;
- station/reverse through the underlying body motions.

These moving relations are primary sources of Intersections.

---

## Relationship-Field Synchronic Spine

A distinct pair chronology.

First:

```text
AB = Composite(A,B)
```

Then:

```text
S_AB(t) = Composite(AB,M(t))
```

This answers:

> What is the relationship Field itself doing through time?

It must not be collapsed into `R_AB(t)`, which asks how A's and B's separate synchronic Fields meet.

# V. TEMPORAL ORGANIZATION

## Moment Lock

The instant capture gesture that fixes NOW before interpretation/classification.

```text
NOW -> LOCK -> Crystal
```

A question, note, tag, Field link, or Horary topic may be added later without altering the capture time.

---

## Pin

The retention gesture for a moment/Field State already under the cursor or already being read.

Current Orbo already has a Pin mechanism and memory store.

```text
LOCK = capture NOW
PIN  = keep THIS addressed moment
```

Pin is a likely migration path into the common Crystal model.

---

## Log

The richer reflective capture gesture proposed by the Field Journal.

A Log retains/references a Crystal and opens space for:

- activity/kind;
- signed lived rating;
- note;
- source reading;
- outcome/evidence.

The Field Journal roadmap settled a signed rating:

```text
-2 ... +2
went against me -> went for me
```

and preserved Pin as the fast silent gesture.

---

## Journal Evidence

User/life evidence attached to a Crystal.

It can include what happened, how it landed, notes, attachments/references, and predicted-vs-felt comparison.

Journal Evidence does not own or redefine the celestial state.

A frozen copy of what Orbo displayed/predicted may be preserved as historical evidence of the instrument's judgment at the time.

---

## Thread

An ordered meaningful set of Crystal references.

```text
C1 -> C2 -> C3 -> C4
```

Optional edges may include:

```text
response_to
caused_by
continuation_of
same_series
```

A Thread is not automatically a Field and does not automatically receive its own spine.

---

## Span

A bounded interval defined by temporal hinges, usually Crystals.

```text
start Crystal ================= end Crystal
```

Examples:

- life;
- pregnancy hypothesis;
- ritual;
- trip;
- production;
- final year;
- communication episode.

Do not confuse the retained **Span object** with Lunar Port's `SPAN` presentation template. One is data/organization; the other is a plate for displaying interval-shaped readings.

---

# VI. FAVORITE

## Favorite

A user instruction that a Field/Crystal matters enough for Orbo to keep it computationally warm according to the object's temporal nature.

> **Favorite is computational commitment, not one universal build recipe.**

Current Connectome persistence already distinguishes favorited chart Expressions from transient moment Expressions.

---

## Favorite Build Policy

The policy selected by the kind/temporal nature of the favorited object.

### Natal Field

May warrant:

- persistent/warm Connectome;
- Synchronic Spine coverage;
- pre-birth/posthumous extension on demand;
- pair coverage;
- visual/iCal timeline windows.

### Relationship Field

May warrant:

- persistent/warm relationship Connectome;
- relationship-field Synchronic Spine;
- optional parent Synchronic Synastry coverage.

### Origin-Anchored Persistent Field

Company/team/nation/project/institution/etc.

May warrant context-appropriate temporal coverage around its relevant history, extended on demand.

### Finite Event Crystal

May warrant:

- persistent AstroDNA/state/provenance;
- warm Connectome;
- Lots/fine structure;
- fast Horary/event/synchronic reads;
- Thread/Span links.

It does **not** receive an independent century timeline merely because it was favorited.

### Thread / Span

May retain organization and cached bounded queries without becoming independent Fields/spines.

# VII. DEATH AND TEMPORAL ROLE

## Death Crystal

A Crystal whose event family is death and whose temporal role marks the end of embodied biography for a human Field.

Death may:

- close a biographical Span;
- anchor final-year research;
- begin a posthumous aftermath Span;
- become a major relational Crystal for surviving Fields.

> **Death closes biography, not synchronic addressability.**

Classifying death does not imply astrological causation or interpretation.

---

## Temporal Role

Metadata describing how a Crystal relates to another Field's lifecycle/history.

Possible provisional values include:

```text
pre_origin
conception_candidate
prenatal
birth_or_origin
embodied_or_active_period
death_or_closure
posthumous_or_post_closure
future_relative_to_observer
external_historical_relation
unknown
```

Temporal relevance and physical participation are separate facts.

# VIII. MOTION AND FINE STRUCTURE

## Applying Aspect

A relation whose current separation is moving toward exact perfection.

Existing v1 definition remains valid.

Architectural amendment:

> applying/separating belongs primarily to the **relation edge**, because it describes how the relation between two moving positions is changing.

---

## Separating Aspect

A relation whose current separation is moving away from exact perfection.

Existing v1 definition remains valid.

A relation edge may also expose:

```text
relative angular speed
time to perfection
time since perfection
ingress
egress
re-formation
```

---

## Fine Structure

The high-resolution relational/derived detail used to distinguish neighboring candidate moments after broad field topology has narrowed the search.

Examples:

- Lots;
- exact-degree relations;
- applying/separating;
- boundary proximity;
- selected minor aspects;
- selected secondary/derived points.

Fine structure does not mean unimportant structure.

> **Broad structure chooses the neighborhood. Fine structure chooses the doorstep.**

---

## Lot

A doctrine-defined derived point calculated from a Field State.

A Lot is not an AstroDNA gene.

Its state should preserve:

```text
formula/tradition provenance
sect used
longitude
sign / house where applicable
ruler/governance references
relations / motion where derived through time
```

---

## Synchronic Lot

**Amended definition.** A Lot derived from the synchronic Field State being examined under the active doctrine.

```text
synchronic derived state
    -> state sect where meaningful
    -> Lot formula
    -> derived Lot
```

**Supersedes v1 research definition:** midpointing a natal Lot with a corresponding moment Lot as the default meaning of Synchronic Lot.

If midpoint-Lot experimentation remains useful, it must be separately named as an experimental technique rather than silently defining all synchronic Lots.

# IX. HORARY AND ELECTIONAL

## Horary

A doctrine-driven reading/judgment of a meaningful captured Moment Field/Crystal.

Horary begins by capturing the temporal address, not by requiring a finished question first.

```text
Moment Lock
 -> Crystal
 -> open Field snapshot
 -> concern/question resolver
 -> testimony
 -> judgment
 -> interpretation
```

Horary is a reader/judgment mode over the Crystal, not a second celestial storage species.

---

## Horary Field Snapshot

A Horary read of the captured Crystal before the concern is mature enough to force a quested house or yes/no judgment.

It can inspect the moment honestly without manufacturing a question.

---

## Horary Question Judgment

The later mode in which a sufficiently explicit concern is routed through matter-house/significators and the full testimony/judgment architecture.

The question can mature without changing the captured Crystal.

---

## Electional Search

The inverse temporal operation of searching existing Field/Spine conditions for a temporal coordinate suited to a desired action/posture.

```text
HORARY
TIME -> FIELD

ELECTIONAL
FIELD CONDITION -> TIME
```

Electional does not manufacture future synchronic states. It searches the states already defined by temporal functions and selects candidate coordinates.

---

## Temporal Posture

The kind of action sought from an electional query.

Not every election is "act now."

Possible postures include:

```text
ACT
WAIT
PREPARE
OBSERVE
ASK
SEND
SIGN
ARRIVE
```

Doctrine/topic rules determine which structural testimonies make a posture suitable.

---

## Electional Field Stack

The set of temporal Fields relevant to an electional question.

For one native:

```text
M(t)
S_A(t)
```

For two natal Fields:

```text
S_A(t)
S_B(t)
R_AB(t)
```

For an existing relationship Field:

```text
S_AB(t)
```

The action doctrine chooses which layers matter.

The result should expose reasons/testimonies rather than hiding the decision inside one opaque score.

# X. CANONICAL DISTINCTIONS

## Field vs. Crystal

**Field:** the astrological configuration.

**Crystal:** that Field State fixed/addressed at a temporal coordinate for use/retention/selection.

They are not competing species.

---

## Crystallization vs. Persistence

**Crystallization:** resolve/fix the state at `t`.

**Persistence:** decide to keep/reference that Crystal beyond the immediate computation.

A state can be crystallized transiently.

---

## Moment Lock vs. Pin vs. Log

```text
Moment Lock
  capture NOW immediately

Pin
  keep the moment already under the cursor

Log
  keep the reading and add reflective evidence/context
```

All can converge on one Crystal model.

---

## Event vs. Field

An event chart is already a finite Moment Field.

Being a Field does not imply that it deserves a timeline of its own.

---

## Favorite vs. Spine

Favorite is a computational-retention policy trigger.

Spine is a temporal structure.

Some Favorites justify spine coverage. Others do not.

---

## Thread vs. Field

A Thread organizes multiple Crystals.

It is not automatically a new astrological Field.

---

## Span vs. Spine

A Span is a bounded interval.

A Spine is the broader temporal function/index through which spans may be queried.

---

## Synchronic vs. Live

Synchronic means Field-to-time relation.

Live/current means the playhead is at NOW.

A historical or future synchronic state remains synchronic.

---

## Biography vs. Synchronic Extent

Biography is the physically lived interval.

Synchronic extent is the temporal range over which the Field can be related to Moment Fields.

Birth and death are major lifecycle Crystals but do not mathematically fence the Field's temporal addressability.

---

## Synchronic Synastry Spine vs. Relationship-Field Synchronic Spine

```text
R_AB(t)
= Synastry(S_A(t),S_B(t))
```

asks how A and B's personal synchronic Fields meet through time.

```text
S_AB(t)
= Composite(Composite(A,B),M(t))
```

asks what the relationship Field itself is doing through time.

Do not collapse them.

---

## Intersection vs. Crystal

A **Crystal** is a retained/addressed Field State at a time.

An **Intersection** is a computed meaningful relational event on a Synchronic Synastry Spine.

An Intersection may be crystallized/retained, but not every Crystal is an Intersection.

---

## Lot vs. AstroDNA Gene

AstroDNA genes encode the primary celestial clock state.

Lots are derived, doctrine-dependent points computed from the Field State.

Both may have first-class Connectome expression, but they are not the same ontology.

# XI. CURRENT CENTRAL PROPOSITION

The v1 glossary ended with the idea that each moment forms a new relational field with the natal chart and two people therefore have changing person-time fields whose relationship can be watched.

The August 15 reconciliation extends that proposition without discarding it:

> **A chart is already a Field: a resolved enclosure/snapshot of light and energy in motion. A natal Field can be related to celestial time at any supported temporal coordinate, producing a Synchronic Spine. Birth is the Field's identity coordinate, not the boundary of that temporal relation. NOW is only the playhead. Crystals are the coordinates that are fixed, selected, or retained because they matter. Two synchronic timelines can be compared continuously; their exact relational events are Intersections. Horary captures a meaningful Crystal after significance announces itself. Electional searches the existing temporal Field for a future Crystal suited to an intended act.**

This is the core temporal ontology to carry into the unified plan.
