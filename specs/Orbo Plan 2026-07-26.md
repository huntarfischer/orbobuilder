## Laws established this session (candidates for CLAUDE.md once each phase lands)

**The depth law.** Reading depth is not three levels of jargon, it is three levels of *inference
distance from the sky*.

- **L1 — what is.** Positional, confirmable by looking up. Sign, degree, phase, next ingress,
  aspect exact now, rising/setting. No doctrine.
- **L2 — what it belongs to.** One step of classical framework. House, sect, domicile ruler,
  dignity, applying/separating, void-of-course, profected house + lord of the year.
- **L3 — what it derives.** Constructed points and chains. Lots beyond Fortune/Spirit, dispositor
  chains, ZR sub-periods, progressions, monthly/daily profections, mansions, antiscia.

**Test:** if a readout requires you to pick a school, it is L3. L1 needs no settings. L2 needs the
one commitment already made (tropical, whole-sign, traditional rulers). **L3 may never render
without its lineage label.** All three research reports converge on this independently.

**The two-panel split for settings.** The **depth dial** lives in ♑ (gears = behaviour). The
**doctrine presets** live on the **back** (maker's side = which school built this instrument).
Different questions, different homes.

**The double helix.** Two strands on one clock, and a rung.

| Strand | What it is | Chart-dependent? | Materialized? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mundane** (Orbo's own) | Ingresses, stations, lunations, eclipses, planet×planet aspects | No — identical for every human | **Yes, shipped as a data file** |
| **Native's** | Transits to natal, ZR per lot, profections, progressions, lot activations | Yes | **Yes, built once at engrave time** |
| **Synchronic** (the rung) | midpoint(natal, sky) — the synchronic composite's own ingresses + ruler chain | Yes, and live | Mostly yes; cASC excepted |

Why the native's strand is computed *and* materialized: **ZR is not pointwise-evaluable.** It is a
recursive unspooling from a natal lot where each period's length depends on all periods before it —
you must build the sequence to know where you are. Same for profection sub-periods. `timespine.js`
already is that unspooler. And the almanac interleaves both strands into one day view: two tables on
a shared axis is a merge, a table joined to a generator is a re-derivation per render.

**Strand identity key = (natal chart × doctrine preset).** The chart never changes — ♈ is *self*.
But sect rule, ZR starting lot, and peak definition (peak vs. Brennan peak — native's choice, already
`zrPeakDef` in state) do. Changing one re-cuts the strand. Upside: two strands can be held side by
side and labelled, rather than one hidden global silently changing history.

**Materialization rule.** Engrave what is eventful and sparse; read live anything that moves faster
than the cursor. cASC moves 180°/day → sign change ~18×/day, ruler handoff ~6×/day → stays live
(already in CLAUDE.md). Everything else on the personal strand is sparser than the sky: synchronic
beads move at **half** transit rate (the half-gear law), so synchronic Mars changes sign every ~3.5
months, synchronic Sun every ~2. The rung is *quieter* than the sky.

**The composite grammar.** Every reading has three slots and the UI currently shows one:

> **⟨left seat⟩ × ⟨right seat⟩ · ⟨how⟩**

The instrument already *is* the first two — plate is the left seat, rete is the right. One operator:
⊕ (midpoint). Six things can take a seat: you · them · the sky at the cursor · you⊕moment (synchronic,
gilt) · you⊕them (natal composite, frozen rose) · A⊕B (the `pp` mint, rose, roster member).

| State | Label |
|---|---|
| transits to you | `you × sky` |
| transits to the natal composite | `you⊕them × sky` |
| the composite against its own parent | `you⊕them × you` |
| synchronic synastry | `you⊕now × them⊕now` |
| synastry | `you × them` |

Nothing needs a special name once both seats are printed. **Kill the word "intersections"** — it is
the only label in the set that doesn't name its operands; "synchronic synastry" already exists in the
law. Prerequisite: plate vs. rete must be *visually* legible — differentiate by **finish, not value**
(same metal, one engraved/matte and one polished/outlined). No hierarchy.

**No Davison.** Confirmed against the code, which already warns that recomputing from the mean jd
"would silently draw a Davison chart." Therefore: **progressed composite = midpoint of two progressed
charts**, `prog(you) ⊕ prog(them)`. Progressing a midpoint composite is undefined — the mean jd is an
identity key, not an epoch. Keep this strictly separate from **composite framing/chronology**, which is
not progression at all but a *scan* of the synchronic composite at the same-ASC moment each day. Two
chronologies of one object; never share a control or a label.

**Composite sect.** A midpoint composite has no place, but it has cASC = midpoint of the two ascendants,
which defines a horizon axis. Sun above/below that axis yields sect. This is load-bearing, not
decorative: sect governs Fortune/Spirit reversal, which benefics and malefics are of the sect, and
which lot ZR runs from — so **composite sect is the gate on running any lot or ZR against a composite.**
Frozen composite → sect fixed once, engravable. Synchronic composite → cASC moves 180°/day, so sect
flips ~2×/day: the relationship has a day and a night. Live property, never engraved.

**Orbo is the herald.** He never tells you anything the instrument isn't already showing; he points.
Voice by depth: silent at L1, names the framework at L2, names the *source* at L3 ("this is Valens's
Eros, not Paulus's Venus-lot").

**The co-rulership law** *(decided 2026-07-28, from the native: "I'm a Scorpio rising, I have two
rulers, Mars and Pluto. Both are valid.")* — **traditional rulership that incorporates modern.**

Every sign keeps its **traditional domicile lord**. Three signs also carry a **modern co-ruler**:
Scorpio → Pluto · Aquarius → Uranus · Pisces → Neptune. The other nine are unchanged. Order is
always traditional first, co-ruler second; neither is "the" ruler and the co-ruler is never a
replacement.

**The boundary is reading vs. computation, and it is load-bearing.**

| Layer | Rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** — both lords named | traditional, then co-ruler | dispositor row · chart ruler / rising lord display · "rules houses" · Orbo's answers · the DPA explain corpus |
| **Computation** — traditional only, always | the seven-planet scheme, sole input | ZR period lengths · profection lord of the year · dignity scoring · electional scoring · rising-lord handoff *timing* · dispositor **chains**, final dispositors, mutual reception |

Why the wall: Hellenistic technique is *defined over* the seven planets. A co-ruler inside a ZR chain
changes period lengths; inside a dispositor chain it mints a false final dispositor (Pluto in Scorpio
would terminate a chain that should keep stepping to Mars); inside dignity scoring the outers have no
detriment, exaltation or fall, so they corrupt the score. **The outers are significators, not
dignity-bearing lords.**

Corollaries:
- **Dignity is untouched.** The co-ruler confers no domicile. Pluto in Scorpio reads *"co-ruler, in the
  sign it co-rules"* — never "domicile", which is a scored dignity term. Detriment stays computed from
  traditional domiciles only.
- **Depth placement.** Traditional lord is L1. Both lords at L2 (the native's own chart must read
  correctly at the default depth). The *reason there are two* — and its lineage label, "modern
  rulership · post-1930 attribution" — is L3.
- **Doctrine preset.** This dual scheme is the **default**, exposed in the Orbo menu as
  `rulers: traditional+modern | traditional | modern`. It is the first preset with visible consequences.
- **The DPA corpus is modern-only** and is tagged as such (`rulers: 'modern'`); where it and the plate
  disagree, Orbo names the source rather than silently siding with either.

Change surface, for when this is built: `RULER_BY_SIGN` (DC 1788), `astrodna.SIGN_RULERS` and
`rulers.DOMICILE` all stay exactly as they are — they are the computation table. Add a sibling
`CO_RULER` (sign index → planet) consumed by the five *display* sites only: `_specRows` (4489),
`_sheetDataCompositeBody` (5704), `_sheetDataNatal` (5817/5825), `_signifRows` (6659/6665). In
`rulers.js`, `lordOf()` gains a `coRuler` field; `dignityOf()` is not touched. Regenerate
`rulers.browser.js`.

## Phase 0 — Device truth — ✅ shipped 2026-07-26 (V0.85)

**Target: iPhone 12 Pro — 390 × 844 CSS px, DPR 3. Safe insets 47 top / 34 bottom. Real canvas 390 × 763.**

Done:
- `viewport-fit=cover` added to the viewport meta — **without it `env(safe-area-inset-*)` reports 0**,
  which is why the already-present `black-translucent` status-bar style was drawing under the notch
  with nothing compensating.
- `:root { --sa-top / --sa-bot }` from `env(safe-area-inset-*)`, applied to the two layers that
  actually carry UI — the onboarding overlay and the `zoomStage` instrument column, both of which get
  `top: var(--sa-top)` / `bottom: var(--sa-bot)` in place of `inset: 0`. **The frame itself stays
  full-bleed on purpose** so its gradient and the starfield/veil layers still run under the notch and
  the home indicator.

  ⚠️ First attempt put `padding` on the frame and was inert — all of the frame's children are
  `position: absolute; inset: 0`, and an absolutely-positioned box resolves `top`/`bottom` against its
  containing block's *padding box*, whose edges **are** the border-box edges. Padding is invisible to
  absolute children; it only moves in-flow content, and the frame has none. Verified the fix by forcing
  `--sa-top: 47px; --sa-bot: 34px` and confirming the ticker logo moves +47 and the stage's
  bottom-anchored elements move −34 while the decorative layers hold at full bleed.

  Side effect handled: `_onbZoomStyle` measured the *frame* to centre the intro closeup on Orbo. With
  the stage now inset, that aimed the camera ~40px off on a notched device — it measures `zoomStageRef`
  first now.
- App icon: Orbo's face, 180×180 opaque square, inline `data:` URI (both `apple-touch-icon` and
  `rel=icon`). `orbo-icon-1024.png` kept in-project for later store use. The wordmark stays the
  in-app mark and is *not* the icon — at 60pt it was a smear.
- Launch image for 390×844@3, solid `#0b081c` so the handoff to first paint is invisible. Carrying
  the wordmark instead cost 407 kB of base64 for a screen you see for 200 ms; the solid fill is 75 kB.
- Manifest inline as a `data:` URI (Android only — iOS reads the `apple-*` metas). **Everything is a
  `data:` URI on purpose:** the bundler would turn a `<link href>` icon into a `blob:` URL, which
  Add-to-Home-Screen cannot read.
- Standalone rebuilt → `archive/Orbo Astrolabe V0.85 standalone.html` (3.5 MB), verified rendering
  with live positions and Orbo's sphere mounted.

Known cosmetic warning, pre-existing: the bundler reports one unresolvable asset, `#n`. Not present
in any `src`/`href` in the DC or the engines; nothing visibly broken. Worth 10 minutes some day, not now.

## Phase 0b — The device box — ✅ shipped 2026-07-27 (V0.85)

Phase 0 shipped the metas and the intent; on device the intent was inert. Judged on a real iPhone 12
in standalone mode, three things were wrong at once.

**The insets were resolving to zero.** `--sa-top` / `--sa-bot` were declared on `:root` and consumed as
`var(--sa-top, 0px)`, and on device the consuming layers were getting the *fallback* — the wordmark sat
~13px from the top instead of ~67px, i.e. underneath the status-bar clock, with "ASTROLABE · V0.85"
under the LTE/battery. The whole 7/26 inset pass was inert on the one platform it exists for. Fixed by
reading `env(safe-area-inset-*)` **directly** in the two layers that carry UI (the onboarding overlay and
the `zoomStage` column) instead of routing it through a custom property. The frame stays full-bleed.

**`_box()` — the device box, sole owner of WHERE.** Same shape as the TimeSpine, for space instead of
time. "390 × 763" was an assumption in a comment while three places each measured their own idea of the
screen: the intro camera measured the stage, Orbo's orbit measured the *frame* (one inset off from the
stage he is actually positioned in), and the eclipse rest walked `offsetParent` with a magic `740`
fallback. Now one memoized read — `{ w, h, inset, head }` — invalidated in `_size()`, the
ResizeObserver's door, exactly like `_eYCache`. Orbo's orbit and the eclipse rest both ask it.

**Orbo skims the chrome band.** His post overlapped the wordmark. Above `box.head` his vertical travel
compresses to 35% rather than clipping, so the top of his arc curves along the header instead of parking
his face on it — a hard clamp would flatten the ellipse into a straight run.

**Also this pass:** astrolabe down to `astrolabeShift: -40` (up 40 from -72, then back down 8 on device
judgement — it obeys the rule of thirds now). Orbo's float restored (`orboFloatAmp` had been dialled to
0, so the bob was multiplying by nothing) and re-timed to a **breath**: `orboFloatSpeed 1.4` ≈ a 4.5s
cycle at 5px, crossfaded on the one `dt` from the one RAF. **He either floats or he orbits, never both** —
the bob belongs to mode `home` (the parked, attentive Orbo, which includes the whole cold open); once
he's in transit the orbit IS his motion.

**The cold open is staged** (the framing half of Phase 5; the choreography stays there). The onboarding
overlay is transparent by design — the night sky behind the splash is the *frame's* own gradient — so
everything in the instrument column showed through it, which is why the wheel's rim swung into frame
behind "tap orbo to begin". One `--instr-op` var now holds the wordmark row, the wheel and the lunar
pane at 0 until handoff, then dawns them up over 1.15s after a 0.15s delay, so Orbo's pull-back begins
before the instrument's light arrives. Fading, never unmounting: the plate keeps drawing and the RAF is
never gated (instrument-survives-everything).

**Deliberately NOT done:** the disc diameter is still width-driven, not budgeted against `box.h`. It was
on the list — the wheel measured ~372 CSS px on a 390 screen with the As badge clipping the left edge —
but on device it reads as fitting, so it stays. If badge clipping ever returns, the fix is a diameter
cap that includes the ±26px As/Ds/MC/IC footprint, not a smaller disc.

## Phase 3 — The Lunar Surface

Renamed and widened 2026-07-27. Was "the four shared components"; it is the same phase, correctly named.
Every *way of looking* lands in a pane, so the pane's material, its rise, and its header/list layout are
one foundation — and everything from Phase 7 (almanac day view) through Phase 10 (composites, library,
synastry grid) renders into it. It has to precede them.

**A · One material: moonglass — ✅ shipped 2026-07-27.** The v0.85 pane was pale and swallowed the plate;
v0.82's read better and the diagnosis was narrow. The ramp ended in translucent **white**
(`rgba(228,234,248,0.28)` at 100%), so the pane's outer band painted a wash *over* the plate: the moon
read brighter than the sky and the figure/ground inverted. Compounding it, the eclipse veil sat at 0.38
— dimming the brass rim and the whole wheel to buy the pane contrast it should have carried itself.

Now: one dark-violet ramp that loses only **alpha** outward — near-opaque under the ledger rows
(rows on solid dark), ~0.45 through the label/bead band at r≈0.94 so the aspect web and glyphs read
through, a ~10px hem of light at the very edge. The pane node's own opaque background is gone; the limb
bloom went from a 250px wash to a 96px edge highlight; the corona from a 120px halo to 46px; the veil to
0.16 (almanac 0.30), which is what brought the brass rim back. All three `paneMaterial` treatments are
now renderings of that one ramp — one source of stops.

**No `backdrop-filter`.** v0.82's plate read through the limb *sharply*, and that sharpness is half of
why it works — you can still see the chart you're reading about. Blurring an 820px circle that moves
every frame is also the one real mobile-Safari cost on the list. Separation is bought by the pane being
dark and the hem being bright. Blur stays in reserve for the ledger column alone, if a scrim can't buy
the contrast.

**Law (candidate for CLAUDE.md): the moon is darker than the sky.** Silver for moon surfaces, brass for
the instrument, gold only at totality — which is what makes the eclipse feel earned. And the veil seats
the pane; it does not dim the instrument to flatter it.

**B · One mechanic: the stop ladder.** "How high is the pane" is currently spread across `state.sheet`,
`state.eclipse`, `_raisedLens`, `_eReadLen`, `_eGeo`, `flipped`, three constants and
`_eclipseTranslate()`. Unify into one presentation-clock object — `this.pane`, sibling to `this.spine`,
never on it. Stops `gone · peek · facts · eclipse` (device-relative, via `_box()`), plus the drag's
rubber band. One door in (`pane.rise('eclipse')` / `pane.rest()`), one read out (`pane.y` + a per-segment
0..1 factor) that `_shadeTick`, `_draw`'s `_eclipse`, the `_eYCache` invalidation and every drag handler
read instead of each deriving its own. Availability is declared by content (`needs:'eclipse'`), not by
caller, which retires the `_eReadLen > 0 || _raisedLens` special-casing; `flipped` becomes just a reason
the ladder resolves to `gone`. Inherits the presentation-clock law unchanged.

**C · Rimmed box + tabula spread — the content rectangle.** The dome silhouette is the signature and
stays, but headers, subheaders and lists live in a straight-sided panel seated inside it: at v0.85 the
bead ring was sliced off at both edges, `NATAL`/`THE SKY` were half-clipped, and the ledger had lost the
BODY / HSE / DISPOSITOR column headers v0.82 had. That panel **is** the scrim from A — a component, not a
gradient. The tabula spread is the 7/25 ♐ layout, generalized to ♍ ♎ ♏ ♐ ♋ ♑ ♒: **items along one edge ·
description field beside them · pin at the bottom.** Build it with a **depth slot as a first-class prop**
— L1/L2/L3 lives in headers and subheaders, so build it after Phase 2's decisions or rebuild it later.

**D · The occulter — eclipse as a public service.** Split the one concept in two so it stops belonging to
the moon. *The eclipse* is a state of the instrument (light retreats, corona blooms, gold first-contact
flares, `_eclipse` feeds `_draw`), driven by one scalar, whoever raised it. *The occulter* is whatever
body is doing the eclipsing: default the lunar pane, but any surface — maker's side, full-screen almanac,
onboarding, synastry grid, a future full-bleed reading — registers as occulter and inherits the rise
spring, limb light, corona, first contact and dismiss gesture for free. Prove it by moving one
**non-moon** surface onto it; the cold open is the best candidate. Per instrument-survives-everything the
occulter is a fused layer: it fails → `_eclipse` sits at 0 and the plate keeps drawing.

⚠️ **The occulter is a MOTION contract, not an interpretive one.** The maker's side may ride the eclipse
spring, but it must not inherit silver or the moonlight depth ladder — brass and configuration stay
brass and configuration, or the sun/moon law quietly dissolves the first time the back slides up.

**E · Cohesion sweep.** One glass recipe shared by pane body, sub-arc pills, the "how to play" tab and
the mark/calendar sheet (each currently invents its own rgba + blur). One limb-light source, so pane
edge, chip borders and rim agree where the light comes from.

**Order:** material ✅ → snapshot ✅ → ladder (behind identical behaviour) → rimmed box + tabula spread →
occulter API, proven on a non-moon surface → cohesion sweep.

**Risks to settle before B:** the moon's darkness value against the brightest plate state; a contrast
floor for ledger rows (it sizes the panel, not the pane alpha); whether the arc-riding labels keep their
curve with an inset or move into the panel.

## Phase 4 — Orbo physics + orientation merge

**Physics — ✅ shipped 2026-07-27 (V0.85).** All on the one presentation clock; no second timeline, no
CSS transitions on gesture-driven motion.

- **Orbit rate** → `orboSpeed` 1 → **0.4°/s** (~15 min a circuit). The slider was stepped at 1°/s and
  capped at 40, so sub-degree rates were unreachable: now min 0 / max 6 / step 0.1.
- **Non-uniform rate — the fix the pace change forced.** His ellipse is **wider than the screen by
  design**: x sweeps roughly −93…499 on a 390px box, so **45% of a circuit is entirely off-frame** (the
  frame clips). At 1°/s that was ~2.7 min of absence and passed unnoticed; at a planet's 0.4°/s it would
  have been **~7 minutes out of 15** — the pace fix would have read as "Orbo is gone". So he ambles at
  0.4°/s while he can be seen and hurries (`orboHiddenBoost`, default **4×**) through the arc nobody
  watches. **The visible pace is the real pace.** Occupancy is read off the device box at the end of each
  tick and consumed one frame later, so there is no extra layout read in the loop and no second timeline.
  Alternatives rejected: shrinking the ellipse to fit would have needed a 0.56× scale and thrown away a
  tuned orbit; clamping him to the frame edge would have him hovering at the margin like a sticker.
- **Startup dwell** → new `orboStartDwell` (default **10s**), added ON TOP of the ordinary quiet dwell,
  so the opening frame is him looking at you rather than already leaving. Armed by pushing the dwell
