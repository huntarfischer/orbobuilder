# Tympan Imprints

## Canon

A Tympan Imprint is one of twelve universal rising-sign structures selected by the Ascendant.

Each Imprint records only what is knowable from the rising-sign frame itself:

- house → sign
- house → traditional governor
- governor → houses governed
- houses linked by a shared governor

It does not yet include natal placements, co-presence, dispositor chains, or interpretation.
