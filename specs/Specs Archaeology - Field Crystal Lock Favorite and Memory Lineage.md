## 1. Status vocabulary

This document uses five statuses.

### LIVING

A behavior or contract exists in the living app or its current architectural infrastructure and should be migrated rather than rebuilt from zero.

### PARTIAL

Some of the intended mechanism exists, or a later plan developed it substantially, but the complete present concept does not exist as one object.

### RECOVERED

The intention is clearly documented but appears not to have reached the living app in its intended form.

### SUPERSEDED

The old idea was useful ancestry but conflicts with a later ruling and must not govern the unified plan.

### NEW SYNTHESIS

The August 15 discussion connected earlier pieces into a concept that did not previously exist under one explicit name or contract.

# PART I. FIELD AND CRYSTAL

## 2. FIELD

**Status:** LIVING concept, old definition SUPERSEDED, current ontology RECOVERED/CLARIFIED.

### Ancestors

`packs/Orbo Field Theory Glossary.md` defined a Field as a relational environment generated by interaction between two or more astrological structures. That document then called people, moments, institutions, events, and other inputs `Field Participants`.

At the same time, the rest of Orbo repeatedly treated individual chart states as fields in practice:

- natal field
- moment field
- mundane field
- relationship field
- synchronic field
- composite field
- field state
- field event

The relational-only definition had therefore become narrower than the rest of the architecture it was trying to describe.

### Current ruling

> **A chart is already a Field.**

A Field is an astrological configuration of light/energy resolved into chart-like structure.

```text
physical Field
  physically existing celestial state at a declared address

derived Field
  state produced from one or more Fields by a declared operation

synchronic Field
  derived Field produced by relating a Field to a Moment Field
```

A Field may meet another Field and produce a derived Field. Relation creates another Field; it does not grant the original participants fieldhood.

### Migration consequence

Do not build a new `FieldParticipant` ontology in which people/events are non-fields waiting for a relationship to make them fields. Persistent subjects may still have participant roles in a relation, but the underlying chart/state is itself a Field.

The v1 glossary remains useful for many technical terms, but its relational-only Field definition is historical.

## 3. CRYSTALLIZATION

**Status:** NEW SYNTHESIS with strong ancestors.

### Ancestors

Orbo already had several acts that freeze or resolve a moving sky into a state:

- natal engraving
- `spine.at(jd, lat, lon)` returning a state at an address
- saved event charts
- Horary records
- minted composites
- pinned moments
- frozen aspect snapshots in memory
- event rows materialized at exact temporal addresses

None of those earlier objects supplied one general word for the act.

### Current ruling

**Crystallization** is the act of resolving/fixing a continuously moving Field at a temporal address so the state becomes addressable.

The underlying Field can contain motion information even though the state is fixed at one instant. A photograph of motion does not imply the photographed body was stationary. Likewise a Crystal can contain retrograde state, velocity, station proximity, applying/separating relations, and other kinetic expression.

Persistence is a second question. A state may be crystallized for computation and discarded, or crystallized and retained because it matters.

## 4. CRYSTAL

**Status:** NEW SYNTHESIS, assembled from several living ancestors.

### Ancestors

The closest earlier objects are:

- a saved Event in the Ledger
- a Horary roster entry
- a Minted Composite as a frozen chart artifact
- a Field Journal pin
- a Timespine event row
- an Almanac bead
- a photo-derived timestamped memory

The current pin is especially close. The Field Journal spec documents a record containing `jd`, name, note, a frozen aspect snapshot, predicted electional outcome, sequence data, and persistence in the memory store.

### Current ruling

A **Crystal** is a Field-state fixed to a temporal address so it can be retained, named, linked, compared, selected, or returned to.

```text
Crystal
  temporal address
  place / horizon where applicable
  canonical state reference / AstroDNA where physical
  provenance
  source confidence
  Field links
  temporal role
  annotations / evidence references
```

The Crystal does not create a Synchronic Spine. It identifies one coordinate on a Field or Spine that already exists as a temporal function.

A finite event can be a Field and a Crystal without deserving an independent timeline.

### Migration consequence

The unified plan should seek one retained temporal-address object that current Event, Horary, Pin, Journal, photo, and selected-election workflows can reference rather than duplicating the same astronomical state in several stores.

# PART II. LOCK, PIN, LOG, AND JOURNAL

## 5. MOMENT LOCK

**Status:** reviewed Horary concept, not found as a living first-class object.

### Ancestors

The reviewed Horary system in the Library already defines:

```text
HORARY MOMENT LOCK
      -> QUESTION / CONCERN RESOLVER
      -> HORARY TESTIMONY ENGINE
      -> HORARY JUDGMENT LAYER
      -> HORARY INTERPRETATION LAYER
```

It explicitly allows an immutable captured moment to remain in `field_snapshot` mode until the concern matures enough to resolve a quested house.

The user's existing real-world workaround is equivalent in intent: preserve the exact clock time first, decide what to do with it later.

### Current ruling

A **Moment Lock** is the fastest acquisition gesture:

```text
NOW -> lock timestamp/address immediately -> Crystal
```

Nothing should delay the timestamp while a form opens, a question is typed, or an animation plays.

The Horary button may invoke Moment Lock, but Moment Lock is more general than Horary storage.

## 7. LOG

**Status:** RECOVERED, planned but not found living.

### Ancestor

`specs/Spec - Field Journal.md` and `Plan - Field Journal + Rising Lord roadmap.md` distinguish the instant Pin from a richer `⊕` Log action.

The roadmap explicitly locked:

```text
Pin -> silent and instant
Log -> opens draft
```

The richer draft would carry source context, activity/kind, note, and outcome rating.

### Current ruling

Log is a reflective capture/annotation gesture over a Crystal or reading.

```text
LOCK = acquire this instant
PIN  = keep this addressed instant
LOG  = keep this reading and tell Orbo what happened / mattered
```

These are different verbs and should not be flattened into one overloaded button.

## 8. JOURNAL EVIDENCE

**Status:** Pin store LIVING; full evidence surface RECOVERED/unbuilt.

### Ancestors

The Field Journal spec is unusually mature. It proposes growing the existing memory store rather than replacing it.

Existing fields/behaviors include:

- exact `jd`
- name
- note field, currently UI-poor
- frozen `asp` snapshot

Planned additions include:

- activity/kind
- rating
- `source: pin | log | photo`
- `from` reading provenance
- note editing
- `⊕` logging from moon-side readers
- grouping/filtering by repeated signature

The later roadmap settled the rating semantics at:

```text
-2 ... +2
went against me -> went for me
```

It also explicitly preserved instant Pin and gave the richer form to `⊕` Log.

### Current ruling

Journal evidence answers:

> **What happened, how did it land, and what was the user observing?**

It does not own the celestial state.

The Crystal owns/references the temporal state. Journal evidence hangs from that Crystal and may preserve a frozen copy of what Orbo predicted/displayed at observation time for calibration purposes.

### Important distinction

A frozen UI/electional prediction is evidence of what Orbo said then. It must not become the canonical source of celestial structure. Canonical structure remains AstroDNA/Connectome-derived under the appropriate doctrine/version.

## 9. PHOTO RECALL

**Status:** RECOVERED/unbuilt, with one important architecture correction.

### Valuable ancestor

`Spec - Field Journal photo recall.md` contains a powerful two-way loop:

```text
photo -> sky
sky / signature -> past photos
```

The photo supplies EXIF time and often place. The system can then relate lived imagery to celestial state. The spec correctly keeps pixels on-device and treats photos as involuntary journal evidence.

### Superseded identity assumption

The old spec recommends snapping a photo to the nearest matching Timespine `eventKey` and using that event key as the memory identity.

That was natural when Timespine rows were the primary temporal ontology.

Under the restored architecture this should change:

```text
PHOTO EXIF ADDRESS
      -> its own Crystal
      -> optional relation/link to active transit, Intersection, window, Thread, or signature
```

The photo's exact timestamp must not disappear merely because it occurred inside a wider transit window.

### Migration consequence

Keep the associative recall idea. Replace event-row identity with Crystal identity plus event/signature edges.

### Superseded part

Phase 7 hard-coded century pair-spine materialization as a universal favorite behavior.

The August 15 ruling is more general and more economical:

> **Favoriting selects a retention/materialization policy appropriate to the temporal character of the Field or Crystal.**

### Current build matrix

```text

  synchronic coverage
  pre-birth / posthumous extension on demand

RELATIONSHIP FIELD
  warm/persist Connectome
  relationship-field Synchronic Spine

ORIGIN-ANCHORED PERSISTENT FIELD

  retain organization and requested results
```

### Migration consequence

Do not rename `favorite` to `spine`. The concepts are related but not equivalent.

Favorite is a policy trigger. The policy decides whether any spine is warranted.

# PART V. THREAD, SPAN, AND TEMPORAL ORGANIZATION

## 12. THREAD

**Status:** NEW SYNTHESIS with recognizable ancestors; no prior first-class Thread record found.

### Ancestors

The behavior existed without the noun:

- repeated email/call/text records in the Astro-Seek corpus
- apology creation/sending pairs
- repeated anniversaries/returns
- Field Journal grouping by recurring aspect signature
- photo recall grouping many observations under a transit signature
- historical sequences such as trial -> execution -> aftermath

Repository search found no earlier `response_to`/`continuation_of` relation model outside the new Crystallized Moments work.

### Current ruling

A Thread is an ordered, meaningful set of Crystal references.

```text
Thread
  crystals[]
  order
  linked Fields
  optional edges:
    response_to
    caused_by
    continuation_of
    same_series
```

A Thread does not become a Field merely because it contains many events.

A Thread may later be explicitly promoted into a persistent Field if the domain actually warrants that ontology.

Examples:


They can naturally meet without being the same class.

# PART VI. DEATH AND TEMPORAL ROLE

## 14. DEATH

**Status:** NEW first-class taxonomy ruling; no equivalent dedicated prior object found in this ancestor pass.

Death previously could be represented generically as an Event, but that loses its lifecycle role.

### Current ruling

Death is a first-class Crystal/event family and temporal hinge.

It can:

- close the embodied/biographical Span

- begin posthumous aftermath research

Most importantly:

> **Death closes biography, not synchronic addressability.**

A natal Field remains mathematically relatable to later Moment Fields.

No interpretive or causal claim is created by classifying a Crystal as death.

```text
S_A(t) = Composite(A, M(t))
```

```text

  sparse/cache/index structures needed for navigation and reads
```

Do not materialize every instant merely because every instant is computable.

## 19. LOTS AND FINE STRUCTURE

**Status:** importance long present; old Synchronic Lot formula SUPERSEDED; new derived-state rule documented.

### Valuable ancestry

The Field Theory glossary already treats Lots as topical anchors and recognizes them in field state, timing, and electional work.

The user's actual Synchronic Synastry practice relies heavily on Lots and fine points to distinguish close candidate moments.

### Superseded glossary experiment

The v1 glossary defines `Synchronic Lot` as a midpoint-field placement between a natal Lot and a corresponding moment/calculated temporal factor and labels that a research area.

That is not the current architectural law.

### Current ruling

Lots are **derived from the state being examined** under declared doctrine.

```text
Derived synchronic AstroDNA
  -> its own derived Sun/horizon where meaningful
  -> its own sect
  -> doctrine-selected Lots
  -> Connectome derived-point tables and relation edges
```

A Lot is not an AstroDNA gene.

It is a doctrine-provenanced derived point whose formula, sect, and source tradition must remain visible.

Do not silently merge different traditions' points that share a convenient English name.

### Fine structure

Use **fine structure**, not "small things," for the high-resolution discriminators that distinguish neighboring moments:

- Lots
- exact degree relations
- applying/separating
- boundary proximity
- selected minor aspects
- selected derived/secondary points

Broad structure chooses the neighborhood. Fine structure chooses the doorstep.

# PART X. DOCUMENT CONFLICTS AND PRESENT RULINGS

## 21. Conflicts that must not leak into the unified plan

### 21.1 Field is relational-only

**Old:** Field exists only from interaction of two or more structures.

**Current:** every chart/state is already a Field; relation can produce derived Fields.

### 21.2 Synchronic means present/current only

**Old phrasing:** synchronic as present-tense/live field.

**Current:** synchronic describes a Field's relation to Moment Fields through time. NOW is one playhead coordinate.

### 21.3 Synchronic Composite is "always live"

`Ledger Tabula Spec.md` contrasts a Synchronic Composite as always live with a Minted Composite as static.

**Current:** one synchronic composite is one sample of a continuous Synchronic Spine and may be inspected or retained at past, present, or future coordinates.

### 21.4 Composite Chronology defines synchronic existence

**Old:** fixed frame protocols / daily samples can sound like the chronology itself is the object.

**Current:** the continuous Synchronic Spine is the object. Return grids/frame protocols are sampling/indexing/view strategies.

### 21.5 Timespine event row is identity

**Old Field Journal/photo framing:** memories can be keyed directly to a Timespine event identity.

**Current:** Crystal identity preserves the exact temporal address. Computed events/Intersections/signatures are relations to that Crystal.

### 21.6 Favorite means century spine

**Old Phase 7:** favorite pair -> century pair spine.

**Current:** Favorite invokes a type-dependent computational policy and range-based materialization where a spine is warranted.

### 21.7 Synchronic Lot is midpoint(natal Lot, moment Lot)

**Old v1 glossary:** experimental midpoint-Lot definition.

**Current:** Lots derive from the complete state being examined, including that state's sect/formula doctrine.

### 21.8 Horary is a separate celestial storage species

**Old Ledger shape:** `horary` sibling kind beside `event`.

**Current:** Horary may remain a category/filter/intent, but the astronomical moment is one Crystal referenced by Horary inquiry/testimony/judgment records.

### 21.9 SPAN plate equals Span object

**Never:** Lunar Port `SPAN` is a presentation contract. A retained Span is temporal organization bounded by Crystals.
