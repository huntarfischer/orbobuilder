# Personal Spine Lifecycle — Forge Authorization

**Status:** FROZEN  
**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Branch:** `feature/arc-v2`  
**Scope:** progression, authorization, and manufacture lifecycle only; no implementation

This doctrine defines when a personal Spine may exist, what authorizes Hephaestus to manufacture it, and how manufacture may be divided without blocking ordinary use.

It does **not** use Orbo's L1–L3 terminology. L1–L3 remains reserved for interpretive depth.

---

# 1. The shipped Orbo begins with one universal Spine

Every Orbo ships with the **Mundane OrboSpine** as its universal temporal substrate.

Architecturally the Mundane OrboSpine is twelve-bodied:

```text
11 celestial bodies
+
Terra
=
12-body Mundane OrboSpine
```

The current native implementation may carry the eleven celestial tracts and Terra Marrow through different structures. That storage distinction does not change the architectural body count.

The bundled product may ship with only a chosen temporal portion of the larger manufactured Mundane OrboSpine. Additional temporal range may be downloadable later without changing the law of the Spine.

The shipped Mundane OrboSpine is universal. It is not native-specific.

---

# 2. Personal temporal manufacture begins only after the Hearth exists

During onboarding:

```text
Hermes
  ↓
Atlas
  ↓
Moirai
  ↓
Tapestry
  ↓
Hestia
```

Hestia's acceptance and hanging of the Tapestry establishes the Hearth for that native.

Before the Tapestry is accepted, the shipped Hephaestus is not authorized to manufacture durable native-specific temporal Spines.

After the Tapestry is accepted, native-specific temporal manufacture becomes legal.

**Hearth acceptance is the authorization boundary.**

---

# 3. Hearth acceptance unlocks the Natal Spine Schematic

Once the Tapestry is hung, the system has the fixed native information required to personalize the universal chronology.

At that moment a **Natal Spine Schematic** becomes legal and derivable from the accepted native state and Orbo's canonical tables/laws.

The exact owner that compiles this Schematic is not frozen here.

This doctrine therefore freezes:

```text
accepted Tapestry
+
canonical Orbo tables / laws
+
Mundane OrboSpine ancestry
        ↓
Natal Spine Schematic becomes available
```

but does not yet freeze whether Lachesis, a dedicated protocol, Hephaestus, or another established owner assembles the final Schematic value.

---

# 4. The Natal Spine is the first personal child Spine

The Natal Spine is the first durable child Spine of the native.

Its governing relationship is:

```text
fixed natal chart
        ×
Mundane OrboSpine through time
        ↓
Natal Spine
```

The universal Mundane OrboSpine carries mundane chronology and mundane relationships.

The Natal Spine records the native-specific temporal structure created by the mundane chronology moving against the fixed natal chart.

Its purpose is to precompute and preserve navigable personal temporal facts that would otherwise be repeatedly recalculated at runtime.

The Natal Spine is not an exotic or advanced optional doctrine. It is the natural temporal extension of an accepted natal Tapestry.

---

# 5. Natal Spine manufacture may begin automatically and quietly

Hearth acceptance is sufficient authorization for Natal Spine manufacture.

The user does not need to understand or explicitly approve a technical operation called "build a Natal Spine" before ordinary personal chronology can be prepared.

Hephaestus may begin Natal Spine manufacture quietly after the Hearth is established, provided manufacture does not unnecessarily block ordinary use.

The UI may expose progress where useful, but progress presentation is not part of the manufacturing law.

---

# 6. Manufacture is divisible by shell, body, or both

A personal Spine does not need to be forged as one monolithic artifact.

Hephaestus may divide work along the durable structures Orbo already owns.

Valid manufacturing partitions include:

```text
by shell
by body
by shell × body
```

The shell system is a primary scale boundary for incremental manufacture.

Current shell families are:

```text
Frame
Revolt
Wave
Zeitgeist
```

Shell partitioning allows Orbo to prioritize useful temporal scale without requiring the entire supported chronology to be forged in one operation.

Body partitioning remains legal where it is the more efficient unit of work.

The Schematic may therefore describe manufacture in pieces without changing the identity or ancestry of the final child Spine.

---

# 7. Quiet manufacture should prioritize usefulness, not completion theater

The lifecycle does not require Hephaestus to finish the entire personal chronology immediately after onboarding.

The preferred behavior is incremental manufacture that makes useful personal chronology available early and extends outward as resources permit.

Possible scheduling policy may consider:

```text
near temporal relevance
shell scale
body
available device resources
downloaded Mundane OrboSpine range
user demand
```

The precise scheduling algorithm is not frozen here.

A partially forged child Spine remains one child Spine whose durable coverage is incomplete, not a collection of unrelated mini-Spines.

---

# 8. Arc is universal even before Synchronic Time is authorized

Arc is a foundational Orbo law and is available to the system in every shipped Orbo.

Arc itself does not require:

```text
AstroDNA
Tapestry
Hearth
native identity
Natal Spine
Synchronic Spine
Synchronic Time authorization
product entitlement
```

The existence and universal availability of Arc do **not** require every user to possess a forged Synchronic Spine.

This distinction is permanent:

```text
FOUNDATIONAL LAW AVAILABLE
≠
PERSONAL ARTIFACT AUTHORIZED
```

Arc may be used by Orbo whenever legal coordinates require half-arc geometry, including outside native-specific Synchronic Time.

What may be gated later is the user's Synchronic Time capability and the manufacture/access of its durable personal chronology. Arc itself may not be product-gated.

---

# 9. Synchronic Time is an unlockable capability

The Synchronic Spine is not automatically authorized merely because Arc exists or the Natal Spine exists.

Synchronic Time requires a later **capability authorization**.

That authorization may be satisfied by product policy such as:

```text
user explicitly enables the Pisces Tabula
product entitlement such as a future OrboPro tier
another future access rule
or a combination of these
```

The architecture must not hardcode one business model.

A paywall, free unlock, trial, invitation, or other product decision may change without changing Arc, Hephaestus, or the Synchronic Spine law.

Any such gate applies to the Synchronic Time instrument and its personal durable artifact, never to Arc's foundational system availability.

The Pisces Tabula remains the natural Astrolabe surface through which the user deliberately enters or enables Synchronic Time when product policy permits it.

---

# 10. Synchronic authorization unlocks the Synchronic Spine Schematic

Once Synchronic Time is authorized for the native, a **Synchronic Spine Schematic** becomes legal.

Its governing relationship is:

```text
Mundane OrboSpine
+
accepted Tapestry / natal anchors
+
Arc
        ↓
Synchronic Spine Schematic
        ↓
Hephaestus
        ↓
Synchronic Spine
```

Arc supplies the law of refraction.

Hephaestus supplies manufacture.

Authorization decides whether the personal Synchronic Time artifact may be forged and exposed to the user. It does not decide whether Arc can answer a legal half-arc query.

These responsibilities must remain separate.

---

# 11. Synchronic manufacture may use the same incremental strategy

Once authorized, the Synchronic Spine may also be forged incrementally.

Its manufacture may be partitioned by:

```text
shell
body
shell × body
```

The existence of a Synchronic capability does not require immediate manufacture of every supported century, shell, body, or derived relationship.

The same principle applies:

> **Knowledge may ship before matter is forged.**

---

# 12. The Astrolabe exposes progression; the lunar pane reflects it

The **Astrolabe is the Sun**: it is Orbo's presentation and illumination surface. Its Tabulae may reveal capabilities, place a legal frame or state under examination, and provide the user's route into deeper instruments.

The **lunar pane is the Moon**: it reflects and reads facts already made true by Orbo's underlying laws. It does not control, activate, redefine, or manufacture those laws.

The foundational laws, including Ring, Mater, Tympan, and Arc, sit between what the Astrolabe illuminates and what the lunar pane reflects.

The Astrolabe and its Tabulae may:

```text
reveal capabilities
request or satisfy user-facing authorization
show availability / progress
open personal Spines
select Chronicle, Clock, Synastry, or other views
```

The lunar pane may:

```text
reflect facts about the illuminated state
read and navigate a Spine
expose deeper interpretation where authorized
```

Neither surface owns the temporal artifacts or foundational truths it exposes.

Iris may visualize availability, progress, or the resulting structures without becoming a source of temporal truth.

# 15. Freeze

This doctrine freezes the following lifecycle laws:

1. **Orbo ships with the universal twelve-bodied Mundane OrboSpine: eleven celestial bodies plus Terra.**
2. **Hestia's acceptance of the Tapestry is the authorization boundary for native-specific durable temporal manufacture.**
3. **Hearth acceptance makes the Natal Spine Schematic legal and permits Hephaestus to begin quiet automatic Natal Spine manufacture.**
4. **The Natal Spine is the first personal child Spine and records mundane time moving against the fixed natal chart.**
5. **Personal Spine manufacture may be divided by shell, body, or shell × body; shell is a first-class scale boundary for incremental work.**
6. **Arc is a universally available foundational law and is never gated by personal artifact authorization or product entitlement.**
7. **Synchronic Time requires a later capability authorization whose product policy may be free, paid, or otherwise gated without changing Arc's availability or the architecture.**
8. **Once Synchronic Time is authorized, its Schematic becomes legal and Hephaestus may forge the Synchronic Spine incrementally.**
9. **The Astrolabe/Sun illuminates and exposes states and progression; the lunar pane/Moon reflects and reads system truths; neither owns the Titans, temporal manufacture, or the truths it presents.**

> **The Hearth authorizes personal time. The gate authorizes deeper time. Hephaestus forges only what the native is permitted to possess. Arc remains a law of Orbo regardless.**
