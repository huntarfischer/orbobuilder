# ORBO SYNCHRONIC SPINE
## ACT II — THE FORGE

## Purpose

Act II takes the **Certified Synchronic Spine Schematic** created and certified in Act I and forges it into the completed **Synchronic Spine**.

No new astrology is created in Act II.

The Schematic is the authority.

Hephaestus forges it.

The Dioscuri verify the forge.

Hephaestus seals the verified Spine.

Hermes takes custody of the sealed Spine for delivery to the Time Garden.

---

# BEAT 1 — HERMES DELIVERS THE CERTIFIED SCHEMATIC TO HEPHAESTUS

Hermes arrives at the Forge carrying the Parcel from Act I.

Inside is the:

```text
CERTIFIED SYNCHRONIC SPINE SCHEMATIC
```

The Schematic contains:

```text
1 Bone
12 Asteria Passes
7 Themis Imprints
3 Oceanus Tides
12 Rhea Qualifiers
```

Hermes hands the Parcel to Hephaestus.

## What is gained

```text
CERTIFIED SYNCHRONIC SPINE SCHEMATIC
IN HEPHAESTUS'S CUSTODY
```

## What is carried forward

Hephaestus now has the complete certified Schematic he is required to forge.

The astrology is already finished.

The Schematic is the authority.

---

# BEAT 2 — HEPHAESTUS FORGES THE SYNCHRONIC SPINE CANDIDATE

Hephaestus takes the certified Schematic and forges its tables into the actual Synchronic Timespine.

He binds:

```text
1 Bone
12 Asteria Passes
7 Themis Imprints
3 Oceanus Tides
12 Rhea Qualifiers
```

into one native-specific temporal object.

This is also where Hephaestus forges the structural anatomy that belongs to the Timespine itself, including:

```text
Door I   Horae
Door II  Chronos
Door III Hecate
```

Hephaestus does not create or reinterpret any astrology.

He forges the certified Schematic into the object it describes.

## What is gained

```text
SYNCHRONIC SPINE CANDIDATE
```

## What is carried forward

The candidate contains the complete forged form of the certified Schematic and its provenance.

It is not yet sealed.

---

# BEAT 3 — HEPHAESTUS SUBMITS THE CANDIDATE TO THE DIOSCURI

Hephaestus gives the forged Synchronic Spine candidate to the Dioscuri.

The Dioscuri compare:

```text
CERTIFIED SYNCHRONIC SPINE SCHEMATIC

↕️

FORGED SYNCHRONIC SPINE CANDIDATE
```

Their task is to determine whether the forged Spine is consistent with the Schematic from which it was made.

They are not recertifying the astrology.

They are verifying the forge.

## What is gained

```text
INDEPENDENT FORGE VERIFICATION
```

## What is carried forward

The Dioscuri now hold both:

```text
the certified Schematic
+
the forged candidate
```

and compare one against the other.

---

# BEAT 4 — IF THE SPINE IS INCONSISTENT, THE DIOSCURI RETURN IT WITH THEIR REASONS

If the forged Synchronic Spine is not consistent with the certified Schematic, the Dioscuri return it to Hephaestus with their reasons.

The Schematic does not change.

The problem is in the forge.

The process returns to:

```text
BEAT 2
Hephaestus reforges the candidate

↓

BEAT 3
Hephaestus resubmits it to the Dioscuri
```

The failure cycle is:

```text
DIOSCURI
find inconsistency
↓
return candidate + reasons
↓
HEPHAESTUS
reforges
↓
resubmits
↓
DIOSCURI
verify again
```

## What is gained

A precise account of what is inconsistent between the forged Spine and its certified Schematic.

## What is carried forward

```text
UNCHANGED CERTIFIED SCHEMATIC
+
DIOSCURI REASONS
+
REFORGED CANDIDATE
```

The cycle continues until the candidate is consistent.

---

# BEAT 5 — IF THE SPINE IS CONSISTENT, THE DIOSCURI RETURN IT WITH THEIR TESTIMONY

If the forged Synchronic Spine is consistent with the certified Schematic, the Dioscuri return the candidate to Hephaestus with their **Testimony**.

Their Testimony confirms that the object Hephaestus forged is consistent with the certified Schematic they were given.

## What is gained

```text
VERIFIED SYNCHRONIC SPINE CANDIDATE
+
DIOSCURI TESTIMONY
```

## What is carried forward

Hephaestus receives:

```text
the verified candidate
+
the Dioscuri Testimony
```

The candidate is now ready to be sealed.

---

# BEAT 6 — HEPHAESTUS SEALS THE SYNCHRONIC SPINE

Hephaestus receives the verified candidate and the Dioscuri Testimony.

He seals the Synchronic Spine.

At this moment, the object is no longer a candidate.

It becomes the completed:

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE
```

The forge sequence has now succeeded:

```text
CERTIFIED SCHEMATIC
↓
FORGED CANDIDATE
↓
DIOSCURI VERIFICATION
↓
DIOSCURI TESTIMONY
↓
HEPHAESTUS SEAL
↓
SYNCHRONIC SPINE
```

## What is gained

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE
```

## What is carried forward

The completed Timespine carries:

```text
101-year Bone

12 Asteria Passes
7 Themis Imprints
3 Oceanus Tides
12 Rhea Qualifiers

Door I — Horae
Door II — Chronos
Door III — Hecate

certified Schematic provenance
Dioscuri Testimony
Hephaestus seal
```

---

# BEAT 7 — HEPHAESTUS CALLS HERMES

With the Synchronic Spine complete and sealed, Hephaestus places it in the Parcel and calls Hermes.

Hermes arrives at the Forge and takes custody of:

```text
PARCEL
containing
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE
```

Hephaestus's work is complete.

## What is gained

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE
+
PARCEL
+
HERMES CUSTODY
```

## What is carried forward

Hermes leaves the Forge carrying the completed Synchronic Spine.

The destination is the Time Garden.

The actual delivery belongs to Act III.
