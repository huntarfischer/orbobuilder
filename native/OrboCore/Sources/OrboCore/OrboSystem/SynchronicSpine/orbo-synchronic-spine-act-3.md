## ACT III — THE TIME GARDEN

### Beat 1 — Hermes delivers the sealed Synchronic Spine to the Time Garden

Hermes arrives at the Time Garden carrying the Parcel from Act II.

Inside is the:

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE
```

He delivers the Spine to the Time Garden.

The Spine is complete as a forged object, but it is not yet planted and it is not yet available to the Orbo system.

Before it can be planted, it must earn three things:

```text
HORAE SCAN
CHRONOS QUERY
HECATE'S BLESSING
```

#### What is gained

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE
IN THE TIME GARDEN
```

#### What is carried forward

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE

Horae Scan          MISSING
Chronos Query       MISSING
Hecate's Blessing   MISSING
```

---

### Beat 2 — The Horae plant themselves at Door I and perform the Horae Scan

The Horae take their place at **Door I**.

They scan the Synchronic Spine from beginning to end:

```text
START
↓
NATAL
↓
END
```

They do not create or alter any Synchronic astrology.

They scan the complete forged Spine and establish that it can be read across its full temporal extent.

When their work is complete, the Spine earns:

```text
HORAE SCAN
```

#### What is gained

```text
HORAE SCAN
```

#### What is carried forward

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE

Horae Scan          EARNED
Chronos Query       MISSING
Hecate's Blessing   MISSING
```

The full 101-year Synchronic Spine has now been scanned from beginning to end.

---

### Beat 3 — Chronos opens Door II and performs the Chronos Query

Chronos opens **Door II**.

He receives the complete Synchronic Spine after the Horae Scan and classifies the Timespine for future Query.

Chronos does not calculate new astrology and does not change the contents of the Spine.

He classifies what is already present so the Synchronic Timespine can later be queried through the Orbo system.

When his work is complete, the Spine earns:

```text
CHRONOS QUERY
```

#### What is gained

```text
CHRONOS QUERY
```

#### What is carried forward

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE

Horae Scan          EARNED
Chronos Query       EARNED
Hecate's Blessing   MISSING
```

The Spine is now classified for future Query.

---

### Beat 4 — Hecate opens Door III and gives the Spine her Blessing

Hecate opens **Door III**.

She receives the sealed Synchronic Spine after it has earned both its Horae Scan and Chronos Query.

Hecate gives the Synchronic Spine her **Blessing**.

Her Blessing creates the ability for the Synchronic Spine to be used by the wider Orbo system for:

```text
CALCULATIONS
+
COMPARISONS
```

Hecate does not create the Synchronic astrology contained in the Spine.

She makes the completed Spine lawful matter for use elsewhere in Orbo.

When her work is complete, the Spine earns:

```text
HECATE'S BLESSING
```

#### What is gained

```text
HECATE'S BLESSING
```

#### What is carried forward

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE

Horae Scan          EARNED
Chronos Query       EARNED
Hecate's Blessing   EARNED
```

The Synchronic Spine has now earned all three requirements for planting.

---

### Beat 5 — The Synchronic Spine is planted in the Time Garden

The Time Garden now has a sealed Synchronic Spine carrying all three required marks:

```text
SEALED SYNCHRONIC SPINE
+
HORAE SCAN
+
CHRONOS QUERY
+
HECATE'S BLESSING
```

With all three requirements fulfilled, the Synchronic Spine is planted in the Time Garden.

This is the moment the completed object becomes an installed part of the native's temporal architecture.

#### What is gained

```text
PLANTED SYNCHRONIC SPINE
```

#### What is carried forward

```text
PLANTED SYNCHRONIC SPINE

Horae Scan          EARNED
Chronos Query       EARNED
Hecate's Blessing   EARNED
```

The Spine is now complete within the Time Garden and lawful for use by the Orbo system.

---

### Beat 6 — Hecate calls Hermes and gives him her Blessing

Once the Synchronic Spine has been planted, Hecate calls Hermes.

She gives Hermes her Blessing that the Synchronic Spine has completed its Time Garden requirements and has been planted.

#### What is gained

```text
HECATE'S BLESSING
IN HERMES'S CUSTODY
```

#### What is carried forward

Hermes now has Hecate's Blessing required to resolve the original Synchronic Spine commission.

---

### Beat 7 — Hermes closes the ticket and announces that the Synchronic Spine is available

Hermes completes both of his roles.

First, as **courier**, he closes the original ticket.

```text
SYNCHRONIC SPINE COMMISSION
→ CLOSED
```

Then, as **messenger**, Hermes announces to the Orbo system:

```text
SYNCHRONIC SPINE AVAILABLE
```

The announcement means the Spine is no longer merely present in the Time Garden.

It is now available for lawful use throughout Orbo.

#### What is gained

```text
TICKET CLOSED
+
SYNCHRONIC SPINE AVAILABLE
```

#### What is carried forward

The completed state is:

```text
SYNCHRONIC SPINE

FORGED
VERIFIED
SEALED
HORAE-SCANNED
CHRONOS-QUERYABLE
HECATE-BLESSED
PLANTED
AVAILABLE
```
