# ORBO SYNCHRONIC SPINE
## ACT I — THE FATES

## Purpose

Act I creates and certifies the **Synchronic Spine Schematic** that Hephaestus will later forge into the Synchronic Spine.

The Moirai do not forge the Spine.

Clotho creates the **Pattern** for the Schematic.

Lachesis fulfills that Pattern by gathering the required Titan-made fields.

Atropos verifies that the Pattern has been fulfilled and certifies the completed Schematic.

The Pattern requires:

```text
1 Bone
12 Asteria Passes
7 Themis Imprints
3 Oceanus Tides
12 Rhea Qualifiers
```

The certified Schematic is the completed series of tables that will be conveyed to Hephaestus in Act II.

---

# BEAT 1 — HERMES ENTREATS CLOTHO

Orbo calls for the creation of the native's Synchronic Spine.

Hermes takes that commission directly to Clotho and entreats her to create a **Synchronic Spine Schematic** for the native.

## What is gained

```text
ONE SYNCHRONIC SPINE COMMISSION
```

The commission establishes:

```text
one native
one Synchronic Spine
one continuous creation cycle
```

## What is carried forward

```text
native identity
+
Synchronic Spine commission
+
request for one Synchronic Spine Schematic
```

---

# BEAT 2 — CLOTHO CREATES THE PATTERN AND CUTS THE BONE

Clotho creates the **Pattern** for the Synchronic Spine Schematic.

The Pattern calls for:

```text
1 Bone
12 Asteria Passes
7 Themis Imprints
3 Oceanus Tides
12 Rhea Qualifiers
```

Clotho completes item one on the Pattern herself by cutting the Synchronic Bone:

```text
START = natal - 1 Gregorian year

NATAL

END = natal + 100 Gregorian years
```

Clotho gives the Pattern and completed Bone to Lachesis.

## What is gained

```text
SYNCHRONIC SPINE PATTERN

1 Bone                 COMPLETE
12 Asteria Passes      NEEDED
7 Themis Imprints      NEEDED
3 Oceanus Tides        NEEDED
12 Rhea Qualifiers     NEEDED
```

## What is carried forward

```text
Synchronic Spine Pattern
+
101-year Bone
```

Lachesis now knows exactly what must be created to fulfill the Pattern.

# BEAT 3 — LACHESIS CALLS ASTERIA

Lachesis calls Asteria to create the twelve Asteria Passes.

Asteria works through the twelve bodies one at a time and uses **Arc** on each body across the full Synchronic Bone.

For the planetary bodies:

```text
natal body
+
mundane same-body chronology
↓
ASTERIA
↓
synchronic body chronology
```

Terra counts among the twelve bodies.

Terra is the universal Ascendant reference: the fixed Horizon at the prime meridian from which local Horizons can be known.

Asteria therefore also uses Terra in the creation of the Synchronic Ascendant chronology.

## What is gained

```text
12 ASTERIA PASSES
```

Together, these twelve Passes form the foundational **Synchronic ephemeris**.

## What is carried forward

```text
1 Bone
+
12 Asteria Passes
```

The new Synchronic matter now exists across the full 101-year Bone.

---

# BEAT 4 — LACHESIS CALLS THEMIS

Lachesis gives the Synchronic matter to Themis.

Themis contributes the seven possible Tympan structures available to the native's Synchronic Ascendant.

They are always:

```text
natal rising -3 signs
natal rising -2 signs
natal rising -1 sign
natal rising
natal rising +1 sign
natal rising +2 signs
natal rising +3 signs
```

These are seven complete **Themis Imprints**.

Each Imprint preserves its own house and governance structure.

The same Synchronic ephemeris can therefore be understood through the native's fixed natal frame or through the active moving Synchronic frame.

## What is gained

```text
7 THEMIS IMPRINTS
```

## What is carried forward

```text
1 Bone
12 Asteria Passes
7 Themis Imprints
```

The Schematic now contains the Synchronic ephemeris and every house framework it can lawfully occupy.

# BEAT 5 — LACHESIS CALLS OCEANUS

Lachesis brings the established Synchronic field to Oceanus.

Oceanus performs three separate passes over the Synchronic matter.

These become three distinct **Tides**.

## Tide I — Inter-Chart

```text
Synchronic ↔ Synchronic
```

This establishes the internal relational geometry of the native's Synchronic field.

## Tide II — Mundane

```text
Mundane ↔ Synchronic
```

This establishes how the universal mundane sky relates to the native's Synchronic field.

## Tide III — Natal

```text
Natal ↔ Synchronic
```

This establishes how the native's permanent natal chart relates to the changing Synchronic field.

These three relational fields can be completely established during creation because all of their source matter is already known for the native across the Bone.

Relationships involving another person's chart remain outside this Schematic.

## What is gained

```text
3 OCEANUS TIDES
```

## What is carried forward

```text
1 Bone
12 Asteria Passes
7 Themis Imprints
3 Oceanus Tides
```

The Pattern now contains positional, house, and relational fields.

# BEAT 6 — LACHESIS CALLS RHEA

Lachesis brings the Synchronic ephemeris to Rhea.

Rhea qualifies **each degree for each of the twelve bodies**.

Her question is:

> When this body occupies this degree, what Mater truth belongs to that body at that degree?

Rhea produces one complete Qualifier for each body.

## What is gained

```text
12 RHEA QUALIFIERS
```

## What is carried forward

```text
1 Bone
12 Asteria Passes
7 Themis Imprints
3 Oceanus Tides
12 Rhea Qualifiers
```

Every item called for by Clotho's Pattern now exists.

# BEAT 7 — LACHESIS HANDS THE COMPLETED FIELDS TO ATROPOS

Lachesis gathers the completed matter required by the Pattern:

```text
1 Bone
12 Asteria Passes
7 Themis Imprints
3 Oceanus Tides
12 Rhea Qualifiers
```

She hands all of it to Atropos.

Atropos verifies each constituent individually.

For each field, she checks that the actual row count matches the row count declared by the Titan who created it.

Conceptually:

```text
Asteria Pass
declared N rows
→ actual N rows

Themis Imprint
declared N rows
→ actual N rows

Oceanus Tide
declared N rows
→ actual N rows

Rhea Qualifier
declared N rows
→ actual N rows
```

Atropos also confirms that the Bone is present and that every constituent required by Clotho's Pattern has been produced.

If every required item exists and every declared count is correct, Atropos certifies:

```text
PATTERN FULFILLED
```

The completed body of tables is now the:

# CERTIFIED SYNCHRONIC SPINE SCHEMATIC

## What is gained

```text
CERTIFIED SYNCHRONIC SPINE SCHEMATIC
```

The Schematic is now lawful forge matter.
